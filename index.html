Instructional Resources

★ Daily Weekday Checklists for Children. These checklists are intended for preschool and elementary aged children and can be used by parents to help their children gain independence with their daily morning and evening routines. I recommend printing and laminating the checklists to use with a dry erase marker and perhaps offering a reward like a trip to get ice cream when the child completes the checklist without reminders, either the first time or for a length of time.

Art and Photography

I enjoy dabbling in the arts. I sew, embroider, upholster, create with clay and attempt to knit and carve. I took painting and photography in college at UNC-CH, studying under Jeff Whetstone and capturing the world with my Cannon AE-1. I appreciate the whimsical and enjoy experimenting with Adobe Creative Suite and Ai. I'm still working on organizing, scanning, uploading, editing and protecting images; this section will hopefully change a lot in the coming months.
